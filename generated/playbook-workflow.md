### 2. Audience estimate: Conservative: 3,600 attendees

_What audience size should we plan for?_

**Why:** 3,600 attendees. 40% below the modelled estimate. Right call for a first-year event, an unproven city, or when the venue deposit is non-refundable. An estimate of 3,600 exceeds all local venues except Austin Convention Center (9,000). That is workable but fragile: if it falls through there is no backup at this size. Consider holding a multi-day or satellite overflow option, or scoping down.
