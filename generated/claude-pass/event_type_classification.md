# event_type_classification

**Prompt**

An internal request says: 'We want to show the Corsair to defense primes and DoD program offices in Austin this fall, ideally with press present.' Classify this into one of: convention, company-hosted, panel, other. Give the classification, then one sentence of reasoning, then name the strongest alternative classification and why someone might choose it instead.

**Response**

**Classification: company-hosted**

**Reasoning:** The request implies Saronic controls the asset, the invite list (named primes and program offices), and the timing — that's an event you build and own, not a slot you buy into.

**Strongest alternative: convention.** If a defense or maritime-autonomy show lands in Austin in the fall window, it delivers a pre-assembled audience of primes, program-office staff, and credentialed trade press without Saronic having to drive attendance — a real advantage, since getting O-6s and SES-level attendees to a standalone event on your calendar is the hard part.

**The trade-off that should drive the choice:** company-hosted gives you full control of the narrative, the demo conditions, and who's in the room — but you own the attendance risk and the cost. Convention lowers both, at the price of sharing the floor with competitors and accepting the show's rules on vessel display, press access, and any on-water component.

**One thing worth confirming before you decide:** whether "show the Corsair" means the actual vessel or a booth-scale representation. An in-water demo effectively rules out a convention hall and pushes you toward a company-hosted event on Lake Travis or the coast; a static or virtual display keeps both options open.
