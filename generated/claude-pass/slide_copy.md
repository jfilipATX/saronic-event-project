# slide_copy

**Prompt**

Write title-slide copy for a Saronic event deck: an event named 'Saronic Fleet Week' in Austin for 3,600 attendees. Give a headline of at most six words and a one-line subhead. The brand voice is spare, technical, and understated - no marketing superlatives.

**Response**

Four directions. All headlines are six words or fewer.

**A. Name-forward (safest)**
- Headline: **Saronic Fleet Week**
- Subhead: Austin, Texas · [Dates] · 3,600 attendees
- Trade-off: Zero risk, zero lift. The slide does no work beyond wayfinding. Best if the deck's first content slide carries the thesis.

**B. Platform-forward**
- Headline: **Corsair and Marauder, Underway**
- Subhead: Saronic Fleet Week · Austin, Texas
- Trade-off: Concrete and product-specific. Only use if both platforms are physically present and operating — otherwise the headline writes a check the event doesn't cash.

**C. Water-forward**
- Headline: **Hulls in the Water**
- Subhead: Three days of live operations. Austin, Texas.
- Trade-off: Most tonal fit for spare/technical, and it reads as a statement of fact rather than a claim. Same caveat as B: it implies on-water demos. Austin means Lady Bird Lake or Lake Travis, with permitting and depth constraints — confirm before committing.

**D. Production-forward**
- Headline: **Fleet at Scale**
- Subhead: Saronic Fleet Week · Austin, Texas
- Trade-off: Speaks to the manufacturing story rather than the demo. "At scale" is the closest thing here to a superlative and may read as marketing to a program-office audience. Weakest fit for the stated voice.

**Two things worth flagging:**

1. **The event name itself.** "Fleet Week" is a U.S. Navy public tradition. A vendor borrowing it can land as confident or as presumptuous, depending on the room — Navy attendees are the ones most likely to notice. If the name is already locked and printed, options B and C soften it by putting the emphasis on the hardware rather than the borrowed institution. If it's still open, that's a conversation worth having before the deck is built.

2. **The 3,600 figure on the title slide.** It's an operations number, not an audience-facing one. To an attendee it reads either as irrelevant or as a boast. Date and location are more useful in that slot. Recommend it stays in the run-of-show, not the deck — unless you have a specific reason to signal scale to a particular viewer.

Tell me which direction you want and I'll write three to five variants within it, plus a matching closing slide.
