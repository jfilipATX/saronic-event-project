# venue_fit_reasoning

**Prompt**

A coordinator is choosing between the Austin Convention Center (capacity 9,000) and Palmer Events Center (capacity 3,000) for an event with an estimated 3,600 attendees. Palmer is under capacity but materially cheaper. Lay out the trade-off in under 120 words. Do not recommend one; present what each choice costs the coordinator.

**Response**

**Austin Convention Center (9,000)**
- Costs: materially higher venue fee, plus larger space to fill — 3,600 in a 9,000-capacity hall reads as under-attended, and you pay to heat/cool/staff/decorate square footage you don't need. Higher F&B and AV minimums typically scale with the footprint.
- Buys: headroom for walk-ups, growth, and breakout/demo space for Corsair/Marauder displays without fire-marshal risk.

**Palmer Events Center (3,000)**
- Costs: 600 attendees over posted capacity — not a discount, a compliance problem. Options are capping registration, staggered sessions, overflow space, or off-site spillover, each with its own cost and attendee-experience hit. Fire marshal sign-off is the gate, not your preference.
- Buys: lower venue spend, a full-looking room, simpler logistics.

The real question: is your 3,600 a hard number or an estimate that could move?
