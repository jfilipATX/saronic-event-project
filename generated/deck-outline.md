## Slide 2 · decision — Event type: Convention

Typically ~5,000 attendees. Widest reach and strongest press draw, but the highest cost and the longest lead time — venue and exhibitor contracts land months ahead. Scale is modelled against Austin market size.

## Slide 3 · decision — Audience estimate: Conservative: 3,600 attendees

3,600 attendees. 40% below the modelled estimate. Right call for a first-year event, an unproven city, or when the venue deposit is non-refundable. An estimate of 3,600 exceeds all local venues except Austin Convention Center (9,000). That is workable but fragile: if it falls through there is no backup at this size. Consider holding a multi-day or satellite overflow option, or scoping down.
